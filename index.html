What is it?

The Steam Frame is a new VR headset developed by Valve, the company behind Steam and many well-known game series such as Half-Life, Counter-Strike, and Portal.

Tech Specs

  • 4 nm Snapdragon® 8 Gen 3 - ARM64
  • 16GB Unified LPDDR5X RAM
  • 256GB/1TB UFS storage, microSD card slot
  • 2160 x 2160 LCD (per eye)
  • 72-144Hz refresh rate (144Hz experimental)
